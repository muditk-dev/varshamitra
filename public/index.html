SYNOPTIC STATE RADAR (PHYSICAL DRIVERS)
6 CANONICAL MONSOON REGIMES (OPERATIONAL TAXONOMY)
Active Monsoon 48.1% Share
Indicators: 850 hPa westerly jet > 10 m/s, RH850 > 75%, high moisture flux convergence.
Dynamics: Continuous onshore flow, Ghats windward precipitation crest, broad synoptic coverage.
Break Monsoon 29.7% Share
Indicators: 850 hPa westerly < 6 m/s, RH850 < 60%, suppressed moisture flux.
Dynamics: Monsoon trough migrates north to Himalayan foothills; widespread peninsular rainfall deficit.
Monsoon Depression / Low 10.6% Share
Indicators: MSLP anomaly < -2.0 hPa, cyclonic vorticity > 2.5×10⁻⁵ s⁻¹, RH850 > 85%.
Dynamics: Bay of Bengal cyclonic depression tracking west-northwest across Central India.
Orographic 7.2% Share
Indicators: Slope gradient > 0.0025 m⁻¹, upslope flow > 0.01, elevation > 200 m.
Dynamics: Mechanical forced ascent along Western Ghats escarpment producing localized torrential deluges.
Coastal 2.3% Share
Indicators: Longitude < 73.3°E, elevation < 180 m, boundary RH > 80%, non-orographic.
Dynamics: Konkan marine boundary layer convergence, shallow offshore troughs, intense maritime showers.
Western Disturbance 2.2% Share
Indicators: Latitude > 20.8°N, vertical wind shear > 30 m/s, upper-level westerly perturbation.
Dynamics: Mid-latitude westerly trough extending along northern Maharashtra border (subtropical intrusion).
